# Two builds: Play and full

Google Play bans some of what the assistant can do. Accessibility may not be used by an app that autonomously plans and runs actions, and broad storage and media access are limited to a few app categories. The Play build ships everything Play allows, with the declarations Play asks for: SMS and call log are allowed because the app holds the default assistant role. Anything Play forbids, such as screen control, goes in a separate `full` build that is installed outside Play. We chose two builds over a Play-only app that drops these features, and over a sideload-only app that gives up Play's reach.

**Consequences:** the Play package id is `local.intelligence` (it matches the Play Console app; changed 2026-11-10 before any upload, nothing was ever published under the old id) and is permanent after the first upload. The full build uses its own id, `local.intelligence.full` (`.full` suffix), and its own signing key, so it is a separate app: it installs next to the Play app, and chats, models and settings do not carry over between them. It is published as a signed APK on GitHub Releases.
