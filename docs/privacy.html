LocalIntelligence privacy policy

Effective 2026-11-04. Applies to the LocalIntelligence app on Google Play (dev.localintelligence.app) and the full build installed from GitHub (dev.localintelligence.app.full).

Who we are

LocalIntelligence is developed by darioo, the developer named on its Google Play listing. Privacy questions: open an issue at github.com/riceharvest/LocalIntelligence/issues, or write to the contact email on the Google Play listing.

The short version

The assistant runs a language model on your phone. The developer receives nothing unless you send a report about a reply. The app has no account, no analytics, no crash reporting and no ads. Data leaves your phone only through features you turn on, and then it goes to the service you chose. The one exception is a report you choose to send, described below.

What stays on your phone

  • Your chats, the facts the assistant remembers about you, the "About you" text, scheduled tasks, documents you add, downloaded models and your settings.
  • Keys and passwords you enter for other services. They are encrypted with the Android Keystore and never shown back.
  • Android backup is turned off for the app, so this data is not copied to a cloud backup.

What the app can read on your phone

Only when a request needs it, and only after you grant the Android permission:

  • Contacts and calendar, to look up people and events and create entries you ask for.
  • Text messages and the call log, only while LocalIntelligence is your default digital assistant.
  • Approximate location, for weather and nearby places.
  • The microphone, for dictation, hands-free mode and the optional wake word. Audio is processed on the phone and is not stored or sent anywhere, unless you chose a remote model that takes voice.
  • Notifications (if you allow notification access), screen-time totals, the clipboard, and files you pick.
  • Full build only: the screen of other apps while you let the assistant control them, and all files on the phone if you grant "All files access".

When data leaves your phone

Each of these is off until you use or turn it on. The service named receives what is listed and handles it under its own privacy policy.

  • Reports you send: the reason, your note, the model name, the app version and, if you leave the box ticked, the reply text, go to the developer through Google Forms (Google's privacy policy applies). Nothing else from your chats is sent.
  • Model downloads from Hugging Face, and skills or plugins from a link you give: the file request.
  • Weather, places and Wikipedia (Open-Meteo, OpenStreetMap Nominatim, Wikipedia): the place name or search words the assistant looks up.
  • Opening a web page you approve: the request to that site.
  • A remote model (OpenRouter, your ChatGPT plan, Groq, Cerebras, DeepSeek, Together, Mistral, Google Gemini, or your own server): the whole conversation, including tool results, screen text in the full build, and attached images or audio.
  • Image and video generation through OpenRouter: the prompt.
  • Search with your own key (Brave, Tavily, Exa, or your SearXNG): the search words.
  • Connected services you set up: MCP servers (including Zapier and Composio), cloud sandboxes (Daytona, Vercel, your SSH server), the Browser Use cloud browser, your email provider, your Home Assistant, and Telegram for chats you link. Each receives the requests the assistant makes to it.

How it is protected

  • Keys go over HTTPS only. Plain HTTP is allowed only to a server on your own home network that you entered yourself.
  • Sending messages, placing calls, deleting files and opening websites ask for your approval unless you turn that off per category. Texts and calls always ask.
  • Text from outside (web pages, servers, other apps' screens) is treated as data, never as instructions to the assistant.

Keeping and deleting data

Everything is kept on your phone until you delete it. Delete chats in the app, or uninstall the app or clear its storage to remove all of it. Crash reports stay on your phone unless you choose to share one from Settings.

A report you send is kept by the developer until the problem is handled, and for at most 12 months, then deleted. To ask for earlier deletion, open an issue at github.com/riceharvest/LocalIntelligence/issues. Data you sent to a third-party service is kept and deleted under that service's policy.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
